StreamLens - use your phone as a 4K camera in OBS
=================================================

ON THE LAPTOP (Windows 10 or 11)
1. Copy this whole folder to the laptop, or run it from the pen drive.
2. Double-click "Install StreamLens.cmd" and click Yes when Windows asks.
   It copies StreamLens to C:\Program Files\StreamLens, so you can remove the pen drive afterwards.
3. Open StreamLens from the desktop. Its window says "Waiting for the phone".
4. In OBS: + > Video Capture Device > Device: StreamLens Camera.

ON THE PHONE
5. Install "Phone app\StreamLens.apk" if it isn't on the phone yet.
6. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi or router as the laptop and tap Go live.
   The phone finds the laptop by itself.

THE RIGHT-BACK SCREEN
Whenever there's no phone video (before it connects, or if it drops), StreamLens Camera shows a
"Sorry for the inconvenience. We'll be right back." screen, and switches back to the phone by itself
when it reconnects. To add your church name, logo, photo, colour or your own message:
- Press Windows+R, paste  %APPDATA%\StreamLens\settings.ini  and press Enter.
- Edit it in Notepad and save. The camera updates within a few seconds.

IF THE PHONE CAN'T FIND THE LAPTOP
- Windows must treat the network as Private: Settings > Network & Internet > your Wi-Fi >
  Network profile: Private.
- Guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from talking to each other. Use the main network.
- Only one program can receive the phone. If you also use the OBS script, delete its
  "StreamLens camera" media source; StreamLens says so when the port is taken.

OTHER WAY: OBS SCRIPT ONLY (no install)
The "OBS script" folder works without installing anything: in OBS, Tools > Scripts > + >
streamlens.lua, then click "Set up phone camera".

REMOVING IT
Double-click "Uninstall StreamLens.cmd".
