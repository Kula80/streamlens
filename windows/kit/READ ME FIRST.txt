StreamLens - use your phone as a 4K camera in OBS
=================================================

ON THE LAPTOP (Windows 10 or 11)
1. Double-click StreamLens-Setup.exe and follow the steps (click Yes when Windows asks).
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.
2. StreamLens opens. Its window shows whether the phone is connected, and tells you exactly what
   to fix if Windows is blocking the phone.
3. In OBS: + > Video Capture Device > Device: StreamLens Camera.

ON THE PHONE
4. Install "Phone app\StreamLens.apk" (choose Update if an older one is installed).
5. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi or router as the laptop and tap Go live.
   The phone finds the laptop by itself.

THE RIGHT-BACK SCREEN
Whenever there's no phone video, StreamLens Camera shows "Sorry for the inconvenience. We'll be
right back." and switches back to the phone by itself when it reconnects. Set your church name,
logo, photo, colour and message in the StreamLens window; the preview shows what viewers will see.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Closing the StreamLens window keeps it running next to the clock, so the camera keeps working.
  Quit it from that icon.
- To remove StreamLens: Settings > Apps > StreamLens > Uninstall.
- Guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from talking to each other. Use the main network.
